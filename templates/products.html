About us How to play Play game

Several virulent diseases have broken out simultaneously all over the world! The players are disease-fighting specialists whose mission is to treat disease hotspots while researching cures for each of four plagues before they get out of hand.

Each turn, current player must do 4 actions. Select any combinations of the available actions below.

MOVE

Location icon only display in the place that you can move to. Click on that icon to move there.

TREAT

Treat button is available when you are in a city that have 1 or more disease cube. Click on TREAT button will remove 1 disease cube from the city you are in.

BUILD

Build button is available when you have City card that matches the city you are in. Click on BUILD button build a research center here.

CURE

Cure action is available if you are located at any research station AND have at least 4 City cards of the same color. Click on CURE button to discard those card and discover the correspond vaccine color.

WIN CONDITION

Players collectively win the game immediately when the cures to all 4 diseases are discovered.

LOSE CONDITION

The game immediately ends in defeat for all players if The outbreaks marker reaches last space of the Outbreaks track
